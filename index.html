Personal assistants now act for a user over weeks and months. They must recall the user's past, follow how preferences change, and decide when to step in.

We collect LifeAtlas, more than 180k multimodal life records with photos, audio transcripts, locations, and assistant chats, from more than 150 users who wore a lifelogging camera for up to three months. On top of it we build LifeTrail, 1,327 multiple-choice questions across 16 tasks in four capabilities. Every question allows only records before its anchor time and is reviewed by the user whose records it uses.

One trail, one question

A request that was right in August is wrong in October

Agentic data engine

Every question is grounded, verified, and reviewed by its user

  1. SampleTask-specific samplers draw evidence from the records before an anchor time.
  2. WriteAn agent writes a first-person question with verbatim evidence quotes.
  3. VerifyLocal checks, a blind review, and a counter-evidence audit filter every candidate.
  4. ReviewThe user whose records are used checks each released question.
